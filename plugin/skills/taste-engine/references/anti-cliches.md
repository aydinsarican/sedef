# Anti-clichés — the look of default AI output

Each of these is fine *once, on purpose*. As a default they make a product look machine-made and identical to thousands of others. If the brief truly demands one, write one sentence in `.sedef/direction.md` saying why.

## Palette and surface
- Cream background + serif headline + terracotta accent ("warm editorial" default).
- Black background + acid/lime green accent ("tech" default).
- Purple→blue hero gradients, glowing orbs, aurora blobs, mesh-gradient wallpaper behind everything.
- Gradient text on headlines.
- Glassmorphism as decoration. On iOS 26+ the system chrome is already glass; extra glass on content reads as noise.
- Dark mode made by inverting light mode.

## Layout and components
- The SaaS card kit: equal rounded cards with soft drop shadows in a three-column grid.
- Bento grids by default.
- Broadsheet hairlines between everything.
- ALL-CAPS eyebrow labels, middots (·) as separators and "→" on every link.
- Default shadcn look (zinc neutrals, `rounded-md`, focus ring) left unstyled.
- Hero = headline + subhead + two buttons + floating device mockup.

## Type
- Inter, SF or Roboto as the *only* face with no typographic intent.
- Everything semibold; no contrast between display and text styles.

## Illustration and icons
- Emoji as icons.
- Generic 3D blob people, floating isometric laptops, "AI sparkle ✨" for any AI feature.
- Mascots that echo a famous brand's character (trade-dress risk as well as cliché).

## Motion and flow
- Three-slide onboarding carousel with stock illustrations before any value is shown.
- Confetti on every success.
- Everything fades up 20 px on appear.
- Skeleton shimmer everywhere, even for 50 ms loads.

## Copy
- "Unlock your potential", "Supercharge your …", "Seamless", "Effortless", "Revolutionary", "Your all-in-one …".
- Exclamation marks in every empty state.
